# Fair Play

> **TL;DR:** Use what's strong — that's smart play, not cheating. But if a
> setting is OP, it gets nerfed, no exceptions. Don't build your whole empire
> around one loophole; build a solid strategy instead.

## A word on exploits & OP strategies

We tune balance continuously — and every so often something slips through
that's clearly too strong: a ruler skill, a tower, a combo that outperforms
everything else by a wide margin.

That's not on you. If we ship something broken, that's our mistake, not
yours. And if you use an obviously strong option, you're just playing
smart — that's exactly what we'd expect.

But make no mistake: **if a setting is OP, it gets nerfed.** That's not the
exception, it's the rule. The more a strategy leans *entirely* on one of
these gaps, the harder the balance patch will hit once we close it. Building
your whole empire around a single exploit is building on sand — those gains
can disappear at any time.

In short: play what's strong, for as long as it's strong. But if you build
your entire empire around one loophole instead of a solid strategy, don't
be surprised when that loophole is the first thing to go.

Thank you for helping us make the game better by showing us where it still
breaks. 🙏

*Retroactive balance changes only affect future effects, never progress
you've already earned — trophies and season results are always safe.*
